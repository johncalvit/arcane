Armor

Define new armor materials or edit/delete existing ones. Weight per piece = area weight ratio × target size × Weight/Mod. Damage modifiers are typically negative (damage reduction); Quintessence can also be negative (interferes with magic).

How this reduction is applied in combat: a hit rolls raw damage from 1 up to the weapon action's Max Damage (scaled by the attacker's Power), then subtracts the armor's modifier for that damage type on the location struck, then the result is capped at the weapon's Max Damage again. That means a strong attacker whose raw roll already lands near or above Max Damage can punch through most or all of an armor's reduction — the cap re-asserts itself before armor gets to matter. Armor is most protective against weaker or unlucky rolls, and against attacks with a low Max Damage relative to the armor's modifier; it does less to blunt a heavy hitter's best swings. If a hit "shows no reduction," check the roller's damage line — it prints the raw roll, the armor subtraction, and whether the Max Damage cap kicked in.

Spells

Define spells for casters to learn. Casting Time is whole rounds (0 = Instant). Cost is per cast, or per round while Maintained is checked. Difficulty is the Logic/Intuition/Knowledge value needed for a ~90% success chance — falling short escalates failure risk. Area/AOE targeting, Attack damage, and Creation-type spells (a persistent object) are captured here but not all mechanically active yet — see the spell's own notes.
